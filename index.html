Our Story

Not just a salon.
That Place.

Walk through the door at 42 N Main and you'll feel it instantly — this isn't your average salon. Owned and run by Evy, That Place has become the kind of spot where clients don't just come for great hair or nails. They come back because it genuinely feels like hanging out with longtime friends.

Whether you're here for a custom nail set, a fresh color, lash extensions, or intricate braids, Evy and the team bring creativity, warmth, and real skill to every service. Personalized consultations come standard — because you deserve results made for you.
